How to play

Four forest animals must each find their food, grab it together, and get out before the clock runs out. You win or lose as a team.

Three steps

  • Forage – bring each animal to its own food square.
  • Grab – when all four stand on their food at once, the food is taken automatically and the burrows close.
  • Escape – bring all four animals to the exit.

One phone, split actions

Only the holder plays, and each player has only some actions. Every game the actions are dealt at random: each player gets exactly 2 of the 4 directions, and the other actions (🧭 Explore, 🪵 Climb, 🕳️ Burrow, and 💣 Destroy with hunting towers) are shared out between you. The Ready screen shows who holds what.

Tap Pass and hand over the phone. The clock never stops.

Controls

  • Tap an animal, then tap a lit square to move there. Animals slide in a straight line and are blocked by walls, bushes and each other.
  • 🧭 Explore: an animal on a doorway of its own colour reveals the next tile. Tiles are made on the spot and can be anything from 3×3 to 7×7.
  • 🪵 Climb: from one end of a log to the other.
  • 🕳️ Burrow: from anywhere, jump the animal to any revealed burrow of its own colour. Not possible after the grab.
  • Drag to pan, pinch to zoom, ⤢ to fit the map. Long-press a square or animal to leave a ping.

Timer squares ⏳

Stopping on one resets the clock to the full time limit and swaps the two action sets. Talk freely until the next move.

Extras (optional, chosen in setup)

  • 🪶 Flyer gates: gaps in fences that only the bird and squirrel can cross. They are shortcuts, never the only way.
  • 🚪 One exit each: every animal must leave through the exit of its own colour. Exits open once all of them have been found.
  • 🗼 Hunting towers: the monkey must stand on each tower and use 💣 Destroy. Nobody can leave until they are all gone.

Silence

No talking, pointing or gesturing about the game. Use pings. The only exception is talk time after a timer square.
